# Companion visual baseline

The generic Companion contract has three separate surfaces:

1. Top island: state-specific Q art.
2. Desktop pet: the same Q-art family, proportionally fitted and bottom-aligned.
3. Control panel: state-specific full-proportion portraits.

At 100% scale, the visible bottom alpha of the desktop-pet image should sit almost against the fixed metrics strip. Do not vertically center a wide Q image and do not use a portrait in a desktop-pet slot. Check at least one wide and one tall source image after packaging.
